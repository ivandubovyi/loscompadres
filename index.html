Our Story

We've been here since 2006 — and we're not going anywhere

Los Compadres started as a small family spot in Meadowood Plaza, and that's still exactly what it is. Same neighborhood, same recipes, same people. We don't cut corners — tortillas are pressed fresh each morning, meats marinate overnight, and the salsas are made in-house.


Oh, and before you leave — try the fried ice cream. People drive across town for it.
