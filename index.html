Our Story

Where Every Meal
Is a Celebration

Tio Taco + Tequila brings an exciting and vibrant atmosphere to Christiana Mall. We serve authentic Mexican food with a modern twist — bold ingredients, creative cocktails, and a menu designed to share and savor.

From our world-class tequila selection to signature dishes like Pork Belly Nachos and Coconut Mango Shrimp, every visit is a fiesta. Featured in the Wall Street Journal, New York Post, and named among the best in the region.
